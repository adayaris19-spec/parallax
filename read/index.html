It does not answer questions. It tells you whether a claim is still standing.

Every abstract PARALLAX retrieves is read and given a stance toward your claim: does this record support it, contest it, or neither. Then every supporter is held against every contester.

Forty abstracts is an afternoon of reading. Twenty supporters against thirty contesters is six hundred comparisons, and the handful that actually collide are the only ones that matter. That is the part that does not scale by hand.
