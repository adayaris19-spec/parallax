One instruction. Four things happen.

You state a claim — not a question. Everything after that runs on its own.

01Sweep

124 passes across 18 archives, sampled once per era window rather than once overall, so a founding paper comes back beside this year’s preprint.

02Read twice

Every abstract gets a stance toward your claim — supports, contests, neither — and the position that paper itself backs, in twelve words.

03Cross-check

Those positions cluster into around a hundred distinct claims. Yours is held against all of them, and every supporter against every contester.

04Keep watching

The claim stays tracked. Re-check it and you get the difference: holding, contested, breaking — and the date it turned.

What a reading list cannot give you.

Forty abstracts is an afternoon. These three findings need the whole perimeter held at once, which is the part that does not scale by hand.
