Instrument one

The Record

Pointed at everything humanity has written down. State a claim — not a question — and it tells you whether the claim is still standing.

  • Every abstract read twice: for your claim, and for the claim it makes itself
  • ~100 positions harvested per sweep, none of them typed by anyone
  • The pairs that cannot both be right, with the numbers on each side
  • Holding → contested → breaking, tracked, with the date it turned

What both are doing.

Looking for the same failure from two directions: the place where what is written down, what is predicted, and what is measured stop agreeing with each other.

01Sweep wide

124 passes across 18 archives, sampled once per era window rather than once overall, so a founding paper comes back beside this year’s preprint.

02Read for position

Every record gets a stance toward the claim in hand, and the claim it backs itself — which is how a hundred positions come out of a sweep nobody typed.

03Find the disagreement

Every supporter held against every contester; the pairs that measure the same quantity and differ are the ones that cannot both be right.

04Keep watching

Nothing is a one-off run. Claims stay tracked and orbits keep propagating, so the answer you got in March is not the answer you get in August.
