The one asteroid where the paperwork has been graded. Humanity spent a decade predicting this rock from telescopes and assumption chains, then went and collected 121.6 grams of the answer. That makes Bennu the calibration object for every dossier that follows: the place where you can check whether the way we infer composition actually works.

What is known, by how it is known

measured Orbit — radar and optical astrometry across three apparitions; among the best-determined orbits of any small body. Arecibo · Goldstone · optical surveys, 1999–2018
measured Shape and size — mean diameter 490.06 ± 0.16 m from laser altimetry in orbit.OSIRIS-REx OLA · Barnouin et al. 2019
measured Bulk density — 1.190 ± 0.013 g/cm³ from the spacecraft-mapped gravity field: a rubble pile, roughly half empty space. Scheeres et al. 2019
measured Global hydrated clays — the 2.7-µm absorption everywhere on the surface; water-bearing minerals confirmed on arrival. OVIRS · Hamilton et al. 2019
measured The sample inventory — 33 amino acids, all five nucleobases, abundant ammonia, eleven sodium-bearing evaporite minerals including trona. Glavin et al. 2025 · McCoy et al. 2025
calculated Mass before arrival — from the Yarkovsky drift: sunlight re-radiated by the spinning rock nudges the orbit; the nudge gives the mass. Chesley et al. 2014
calculated Impact probability — 0.037 % cumulative for 2178–2290, the single most-watched date 24 September 2182. Farnocchia et al. 2021
assumed The meteorite analog — every pre-arrival composition estimate rested on “B-type spectra look like CI/CM carbonaceous chondrites.” The sample largely honoured it — but it was an assumption for a decade, and for every unvisited rock it still is. Clark et al. 2011
proposed An ancient brine parent body — the evaporite sequence reads as a salt lake bed on the parent asteroid, wet within the first few million years of the solar system. McCoy et al. 2025 — consistent, not yet unique

The chain that broke is the finding. Thermal inertia was read through an assumption — smooth temperature response means fine grains — that fails on rubble piles. Every thermal-inertia grain-size estimate in the catalog inherits that exposure, and the dossier of every unvisited asteroid now says so.

What would de-risk it next

observation Cross-laboratory chirality replication on pristine aliquots — the abiotic verdict rests on racemic mixtures; an independent lab on an unopened sample settles it beyond argument.
observation Apophis, April 2029 — an S-type passing inside the geostationary belt with a spacecraft (OSIRIS-APEX) already en route: the comparison point for the whole silicate side of the catalog.
observation Psyche, August 2029 — first arrival at a metal-class asteroid; the end-member every “metal-rich” valuation chain is silently assuming.

What the record actually holds

AliquotOSIRIS-REx returned material, from the 121.6 g curated at NASA Johnson
LaboratoryNASA Goddard astrobiology laboratory

Fourteen of the twenty protein-building amino acids are present — 33 amino acids in all. supportedmeasured
Chainsame aliquots, laboratory, technique and paper as above
Contextamino acids in carbonaceous meteorites have been solid since Murchison fell in 1969 (Kvenvolden et al. 1970) — Bennu’s are the first from a sample that never touched weather, ground or a museum drawer
The chemistry is abiotic — no sign of life, past or present. supportedcalculated
Basisthe amino acids are racemic — equal left- and right-handed forms. Life picks a hand; rock chemistry does not. The nitrogen and ammonia enrichment points at cold outer-solar-system chemistry, not biology.
Life’s ingredients arrived from space before life began. proposed
Standingconsistent with the record — asteroids demonstrably carry and deliver the inventory — but not established by it. This is the claim the headlines were reaching for; the honest label is proposed.

The era ladder

The question is older than spectroscopy, and the answer changed this decade. A relevance sort buries all but the last column; the ladder is why the dossier holds all five.

1834Berzelius distills fragments of the Alais stone, finds carbon compounds, and asks the question.
1970Murchison: extraterrestrial amino acids established from a fresh fall.
2008Carbon isotopes prove Murchison’s nucleobases formed off Earth.
2023Uracil in Ryugu — first nucleobase from a pristine returned sample.
2025Bennu: all five bases, 14 protein amino acids, an ancient brine. Racemic throughout.
