The method, already proved once

Uranus was found in 1781. Within forty years its orbit refused to match prediction. That disagreement was the only evidence anyone had, and in 1846 a telescope was pointed where the arithmetic said an unseen planet must be. Neptune was there. Here is that same calculation, run live — with Neptune, and without.

Drag it to zero and the anomaly disappears — in that universe there is nothing out there to cause it. Both searches use the same engine: velocity Verlet N-body, 4- and 8-day steps, re-run on every change. Nothing here is stored or simulated.
