PARALLAX

Instrument one

The Record

It does not answer questions. You give it a claim — and it tells you whether the claim is still standing. Every abstract it retrieves is read and given a stance toward your claim, then every supporter is held against every contester.

  • Twenty supporters against thirty contesters is 600 comparisons — the part that doesn’t scale by hand
  • It surfaces the pairs that cannot both be right, with the numbers on both sides
  • A claim stays tracked. Re-check it and you get what moved
  • Holding → contested → breaking, with the date it turned

Instrument two

The Sky

Pointed at the solar system itself. A live orbital model computed in your browser — sixteen bodies propagated from published elements — whose real job is catching the moment the model and the sky disagree.

  • Run time fifty years forward and watch everything move
  • The Neptune discovery, recomputed live by real N-body integration
  • Claims put on trial by turning them into predictions and checking
  • Every number it runs on, movable — see what breaks
